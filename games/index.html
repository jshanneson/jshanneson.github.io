About me

Hi, I’m Joshua! I’m an Edmonton-based indie developer with a background in 3D modeling, music composition, programming and UX design. I’ve shipped several titles, including Acorn Island and A Crabby Adventure, and released my first commercial game, Ghostie Town.

I have a background in engineering, worked at two start-ups, designed websites and UX flows, written documentation at a bank, made sales cold calls, and managed students and contractors.

Skills

I design and build games, and I’ve lived the production and launch side of shipping one on Steam. On a small team that means one person who can design a feature, scope it realistically and help get the game in front of players.

Game design

Level design, UX flows and user personas, and a three-act narrative for Ghostie Town, all scoped to finish on-time for a Halloween launch.

Production

Kanban and agile, Git workflows on game jams, and experience managing students and contractors. I plan to a ship date and cut scope to hit it.

Launch & community

Steam page and capsule art, Next Fest, bundles, virtual playtests, streamer outreach and a year of social content: 300+ sales and 2,400+ wishlists on my first release.
