About me

A developer who ships, and can also help sell it

I’m an Edmonton-based indie developer with a background in 3D modeling, music composition, programming and UX design. I’ve shipped several titles, including Acorn Island and A Crabby Adventure, and released my first commercial game, Ghostie Town.

Before games I studied engineering, worked at two start-ups, designed websites and UX flows, wrote documentation at a bank, made sales cold calls and managed students and contractors.
